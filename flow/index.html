01 / The cast

Five people.
One source of truth.

Each perspective is a self-contained HTML page. Open one, switch through the live-looking screens, then follow its node-by-node journey.

Admin and officer are deliberately different: the admin owns access and lifecycle; the officer runs the wing day to day.

01System owner

Super Admin

SUPER_ADMIN

Owns the unit's access, data boundaries and audit evidence. The only role that can deactivate a cadet or mint another super admin.

  • Provision staff and cadet logins
  • Full wing scope · all PII
  • Deactivate, audit, annual rollover
Full wingOpen dossier →
02Unit operator

Officer / ANO · CTO

OFFICER_ANO_CTO

Runs enrolment, parades, reports and official communication. Sees the whole wing and is the only non-admin role allowed to export personal contact data.

  • Bulk enrolment and cadet records
  • Wing/platoon parades and locked-session overrides
  • Audited Excel, CSV and PDF exports
Full wingOpen dossier →
03Platoon leader

Senior Under Officer

LEADER_SUO

A cadet leader with a staff console, hard-scoped to the platoon linked to their own cadet record. Fast marking, platoon reports, no sensitive exports.

  • Own-platoon roster and attendance
  • Wing parades included in reporting
  • Can post notices · cannot withdraw them
Platoon scopeOpen dossier →
04Cadet / client

Cadet

CADET

The end user. Sees their complete own record, attendance health, consent status, official notices and the public camp calendar — read-only by design.

  • Own attendance, certificate and contacts
  • Urgent announcements and events
  • Cannot browse the wing register
Own recordOpen dossier →
05Open audience

Public Visitor

NO LOGIN

Parents, prospective cadets, alumni and the university. Discovers the unit, reads published notices and camps, then contacts the office or signs in if issued credentials.

  • Public news, events and notices
  • Organisation, about and contact
  • No personal data · no register access
Published onlyOpen dossier →

“Client” in this guide means the cadet end user (plus the public audience). The unit's client is the Anna University NCC office; the portal's job is to serve both.

Layer 01 / UI

Next.js 14

Feature folders, server-rendered public pages, role-aware client islands and a single API wrapper.

Layer 02 / API

Express + Zod

Thin controllers, fat services, RBAC middleware and a second scope check inside the service layer.

Layer 03 / Data

Postgres + Prisma

One schema for users, cadets, parades, records, notices, news, camps and refresh tokens.

Layer 04 / Evidence

Audit log

Creates, edits, exports, overrides and sign-in events record actor, action, IP and filters.

Honesty note

This is a UX prototype, not the running portal. Screens are populated with representative mock data and every “action” is simulated locally. Capabilities marked live exist in the current MVP; api-ready, data-only and phase 2 are labelled so the team lead can separate today's build from the proposal.
