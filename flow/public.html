The unit / About

Unity and discipline.

The Anna University NCC Army Wing trains cadets in drill, weapon training, map reading, social service and leadership, feeding RDC / CATC / TSC selections and the B/C certificate exams.

The unit

NCC at Anna University is part of the national cadet organisation. The Army Wing parades at the Kottur ground and operates from the NCC Office on campus. Training is weekly, with camps and selections through the year.

Vision: disciplined, service-minded graduates ready for the Armed Forces, civil services and industry. The motto is Unity and Discipline.

Public boundary

The story, the roles and the camp calendar are public. The cadet register, attendance, contact details and certificate records are not — they require a provisioned role and a server-resolved scope.

What waits behind the gate

Four roles. One source of truth.

The same login resolves a different, server-enforced scope. The public site never decides what you can see.

  • Cadet — own record, attendance standing, notices.
  • Platoon leader — one platoon, parade marking, publish-only notices.
  • Officer — whole wing, register, exports, overrides.
  • Super admin — access, deactivation, governance.
Open the field guide

This is a static demo. The real login returns a short-lived access token and a rotating refresh token.

Public registration is disabled by default. An unlinked cadet login is a valid state: the portal tells the person to ask the office to link their register number.

Phase 2

Can access

  • Read the unit story, roles, news, events and public notices.
  • See the contact address and email the office.
  • Open the login form and sign in with provisioned credentials.
  • Browse the gallery shell and understand the consent policy.
  • Share any public page; no login is required.

Cannot guardrails

  • See the cadet register, attendance, certificates or any personal data.
  • Create an account — public registration is disabled by default.
  • Read staff dashboards, reports, exports or the audit trail.
  • Post, edit or withdraw announcements, news or events.
  • Submit a contact form — no endpoint or UI exists.
  • Upload or view unconsented photos.
  • Reset a password; there is no self-service flow yet.

04 / Evidence & gaps

A clean public edge.
A few empty shelves.

The public API is simple and already exists. The main gaps are editorial surfaces (no news/event composer UI), the gallery store, and a contact form that was never built.

News

World-readable

Published stories are returned newest-first. Writes are officer-only and audited.

GET /news · POST /news

Events

Camp calendar

Events are readable by anyone and ordered by start date. Writes are officer-only.

GET /events · POST /events

Notices

Public board

The same announcement feed as the signed-in home page. Urgent sorts above general.

GET /announcements

Login

Provisioned only

Email or register number plus password. Self-registration is opt-in per environment and off by default.

Content

Static pages

About, organisation and contact are simple server-rendered pages. There is no CMS and no contact form.

Gap

Gallery & editor

No media store, no consent-flag join, no news/event composer, no map embed, no social links.

Phase 2 backlog
Team-lead note

The public site is the easiest place to over-promise. Do not claim a live gallery or a working contact form. The honest public features are reading pages, reading the board and signing in with an account the office already made.
