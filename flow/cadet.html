02 / What they can do

Know your standing.
Ask for a fix.

The cadet experience is intentionally read-mostly. The most important capability is the DPDP access path: the person can see everything the unit holds about them, even when staff lists hide it.

01

See your own full record

Name, register number, rank, department, batch, platoon, blood group, phone, email, parent contact, address and consent.

Live MVP
02

Check attendance standing

The cached percentage, the 75% threshold and a low-attendance warning. The underlying parade history is not exposed yet.

Live MVP
03

Read official notices

The public announcement board, with urgent notices pinned above general ones. No posting, editing or withdrawing.

Live MVP
04

Follow news & events

Public stories and the camp calendar. These are the same pages a parent or visitor can read without signing in.

Live MVP
05

Request a correction

There is no self-edit route. The cadet asks an officer, who updates the record and writes an audit row.

Phase 2 UI
06

Download your record

The access path exists in the UI, but there is no cadet-facing export or data-portability download yet.

Phase 2

Can access

  • Sign in with a provisioned AU email or register-number login.
  • Read the full own record via /cadets/me, including contact PII.
  • See the attendance percentage and whether it is below 75%.
  • Read wing-wide official notices, news and events.
  • Browse every public page without signing in.
  • Ask the unit office for a correction or a certificate check.

Cannot guardrails

  • List cadets, search the wing or open another person's record.
  • Read attendance reports, staff dashboards, exports or the audit trail.
  • Edit any field — including phone, parent contact, address or consent.
  • See a parade-by-parade attendance history — no such cadet screen exists.
  • Download or port their record — no cadet export endpoint.
  • Publish, edit or withdraw an announcement.
  • Self-register publicly — disabled by default.

03 / Role journey

Arrive. Sign in.
See yourself clearly.

The cadet's loop starts on the public site and ends in a read-only personal record. The last step is deliberately a handoff to the office, not a self-service form that does not exist.

04 / Evidence & gaps

A real access path.
Not a full self-service portal.

The MVP gets the hardest part right: a cadet can see their own complete record and nobody else's. The self-service gaps are honest and contained.

Access

Own record

The cadet path bypasses staff PII stripping and returns the complete own record. No id parameter is accepted.

GET /cadets/megetOwnCadet()

Login

Register number

The login form accepts an AU email or register number. Accounts are provisioned; public self-registration is off by default.

POST /auth/loginALLOW_PUBLIC_REGISTRATION=0

Attendance

Cached percentage

Cadets see the computed percentage and the threshold flag, not the underlying attendance records.

Cadet.attendancePctisLowAttendance()

Notices

Public board

Announcements are world-readable and ordered urgent-first. A cadet session adds no write permission.

GET /announcementspriority

Public

News & events

The same public content is visible signed-out. There is no separate cadet newsroom.

GET /newsGET /events

Gap

Self-service

No self-edit, consent withdrawal, attendance drill-down, record download or correction request workflow.

Phase 2 backlog
Team-lead note

Do not promise a cadet “edit profile” screen. The current profile is read-only by design and the UI tells the cadet to ask an officer. The DPDP access right is met by the visible record; portability and correction workflow are the next deliverables.
